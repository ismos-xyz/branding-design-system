PERCENTAGE REQUIREMENTS · LIGHT MODE

COLOUR-BLOCK RULES

Every pageAt least one Mauveine block, one accent block (Madder or Weld) and one Logwood 800 block. The home page opens on Mauveine.
NeighboursNever two neighbouring bands, or cards, in the same colour. Paper (Ink in Hacker mode) may sit between two colour bands.
Card fillsMauveine, Indigo, Logwood 800, Madder or Weld, rotating by position. A fill never stands for a tag, level, format or status.
TypeInk on Weld and Madder. White on Mauveine, Logwood 800, Indigo and Logwood. Never white on Madder (2.66:1) or Weld (1.44:1).
Forms and errorsNever on a Madder or Weld block: they sit on Paper or a Logwood panel, so an accent never competes with an alert.
Flat colourNo gradient, tint, transparency or glow. Hard edges, full bleed.

ON SCREEN · LIGHT

  • Paper is the breathing space between blocks, not the whole page.
  • The primary button is Mauveine with white type; on Mauveine, Indigo and Logwood 800 it turns Weld with Ink type.
  • Links are Mauveine, always underlined.
  • Card edges and table rules on Paper: Logwood 200 #DFD8FB.

DARK SURFACES · INK AND LOGWOOD

  • All text on Ink is white #FFFFFF, 21.00:1. Never an off-white or ghost-white tint.
  • Logwood cards on Ink take a 1 px Logwood 700 #6B55A2 edge.
  • Links on dark are Mauveine 200 #BFC3FF, underlined: 12.47:1 on Ink. Mauveine 300 is for the progress bar only.
  • Weld and Madder keep Ink type on dark surfaces as well.

IN PRINT

The same blocks on paper: a Mauveine, Madder or Weld band across the top third, Logwood 800 at the foot, Paper between. Alerts are Cochineal #A8001E, for alerts only.

Status is never carried by hue alone. Every alert takes three cues: the colour, the icon, and the word in capitals, set as a boxed tag. ALERT

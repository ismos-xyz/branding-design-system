Respect Reduce MotionWhen the system setting is on, replace slides, parallax, zooms and auto-play with instant changes or a short cross-fade. Nothing essential depends on animation.

Pause controlAny motion that runs longer than 5 s gets a pause or stop control.
Free orientationScreens rotate freely between portrait and landscape. Lock only when the function demands it (camera capture, a card scanner, a landscape-only game) and state the reason in the spec.

MOBILE APP ACCESSIBILITY BASICS · iOS & ANDROID

Touch targetsMinimum 44 × 44 pt on iOS, 48 × 48 dp on Android, with 8 dp spacing between targets.
Screen readersEvery control has a label, role and state. Decorative images are hidden from assistive tech.
VoiceOver · accessibilityLabelTalkBack · contentDescription
Text scalingType follows the system size up to 200% with no clipping or truncation of essential text.
iOS · Dynamic TypeAndroid · sp units
Focus orderReading and focus order match the visual order. Focus lands on the new content after a screen or modal opens.
Dark and high-contrast modesDark mode uses Hacker mode (3.3). Honor Increase Contrast and High contrast text: swap Logwood 600/700 for 800+ and add borders.
GesturesEvery multi-finger or path gesture has a single-tap alternative. No action depends on shaking the device.
Time limitsUsers can extend, adjust or turn off time limits, including quiz timers.
TestEach release is checked with VoiceOver and TalkBack on, text at max size, Reduce Motion on, and both orientations.

# The palette at a glance

Eight colours in four roles with their V2 shares, and the three minimums that hold in both modes. Part of the Colour section of ISMOS V2 · Vibrant.

- The written rules are in **03 Specification · Colour**, **03.2 Hacker mode** and **06 Accessibility**.
- The card is a reference, not a component to ship: it has no classes in `bundle.css`.

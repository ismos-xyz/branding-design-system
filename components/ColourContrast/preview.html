Target: WCAG 2.2 Level AA on every screen and printed piece. Four principles govern every decision in this chapter.

PPerceivableContent reaches every sense in use: contrast, text alternatives, captions, no color-only meaning.
OOperableEvery action works by touch, keyboard, switch and voice. No time traps. Motion is optional.
UUnderstandablePlain language, predictable navigation, errors named with a fix.
RRobustNative controls and correct roles, so screen readers and assistive tech parse the UI.

COLOR CONTRAST · MINIMUMS

4.5:1

Standard text against its background. Body copy, labels, captions, placeholder text.

3:1

Large text: 18 pt / 24 px regular, or 14 pt / 18.66 px bold and up.

3:1

Essential graphics and UI components: icons, chart series, input borders, focus rings, control states.

02

Principles

Four rules everything follows from.

You can check each one.

1

Make the plainer version.

A student on a phone, at night, in a second language decides in one sentence.

2

Place the artwork. Never redraw it.

The mark, the book and the lockup come from the files.

3

Never let colour carry meaning alone.

Colour + icon + word, every time.

4

Name the person, then the number.

“Catrya is teaching this one.” Then: 8 lessons.

03

Voice

Human, warm, direct.

A calm instructor talking to one person. Not a brochure.

1Write like you speak.If you wouldn’t say it out loud, cut it.
2Keep it human.Tie big ideas to a person or a moment.
3Short sentences.Vary length. End on a punchy line.
4Speak to “you”.Never “learners” or “users”.
5Lower the barrier.You don’t need to know anything yet.
6One idea per sentence.One promise per paragraph.
7No labels.No geographic or demographic categories.
8Rhythm.Fragments and triplets work.
9Close with an action.Or a reassurance. Never a slogan.
Instead of

“Our comprehensive curriculum empowers learners to master self-custody through world-class instruction.”

Write

“Your money, in your hands. We’ll show you how, one step at a time. You don’t need to know anything yet.”

First visit

“Start with a nine-minute lesson.”

Inside a lesson

“Write the twelve words down once.”

Something went wrong

“The connection dropped. Your place is saved.”

Finishing

“You finished Run your own node. Your record is ready.”

13

Research and testing

Design with students, not for them.

Every rule is a guess until students and teachers have used it. Based on IDEO.org human-centred design.

1Inspiration

Who is it for, and what do they need?

Interviews in their language, on their phones. Take a course on 3G. Talk to disabled students and teachers.
2Ideation

What could we make?

Find themes, ask How might we…, sketch several options, prototype rough, get feedback fast.
3Implementation

Does it work in real life?

Pilot with one cohort or one page, measure, iterate, then widen.
DesirableStudents and teachers want it. Ask, don’t assume.
FeasibleIt works with our tokens, on cheap phones, offline.
ViableISMOS can keep it going: an owner, a template, a cost.

Minimum testing for a change

5students per language
3languages at least, Arabic included
2disabled students per round
3teachers in a co-design session

Consent first. Anyone can withdraw. Nobody is named without permission.

What we measure

  • Starting first lesson started
  • Finishing lessons finished, of lessons started
  • Understanding one question at the end
  • Feeling “Was this for you?”
  • Access issues found and fixed

Always with the denominator, per language. Never streaks or scores.

14

Before you ship

Nine ticks.

  • Mark from the file, Ink or white, 20 px or more, clear space kept.
  • Mauveine 28%, Logwood 800 10%, accents 11%, in both modes.
  • No two neighbouring bands or cards in one colour.
  • Body text at 7:1; no FAIL pair used.
  • Every state: colour, icon and word.
  • Literata, Plex Sans, Plex Mono only; no Light weight.
  • Every language written as an original; Arabic mirrored.
  • Every number has its denominator; nothing invented.
  • Tested on a 360 px phone, 3G, both themes.
